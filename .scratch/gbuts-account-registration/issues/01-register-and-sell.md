# 01: Add GButs account registration and sales handoff

**What to build:** The seller can save an existing account or generate account credentials in GButs workspace, mark actual payment and expiry, and open a listing draft for the exact ready account.

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately).

**Status:** in-progress

- [ ] Registration and generator available from GButs home/menu.
- [ ] Account credentials persist and pending accounts are visible without a listing.
- [ ] Paid future-expiry accounts reach shared inventory without duplicate slots.
- [ ] Unsupported services, invalid dates, placeholders and duplicate records rejected.
- [ ] Manual registrations never create/delete a SimpleLogin alias.
- [ ] Correct selected account survives sales navigation.
- [ ] Tests, review, committed deployment and browser smoke verification.
